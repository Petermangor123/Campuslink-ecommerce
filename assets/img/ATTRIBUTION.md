# CampusLink image attribution

Local marketplace photography and brand marks used by the storefront.

## Brand marks (original CampusLink identity)

- `logo.svg` — dark green lockup for light backgrounds (dashboard, light panels)
- `logo-light.svg` — white / light-sage lockup for `#075B45` header, drawer, footer
- `logo-mono.svg` — single-color lockup (`currentColor`) for flexible tints
- `logo-mark.svg` — connected-node symbol only (`currentColor` + sage satellites)
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` — app / tab icons
- Concept: Y-network (hub + three linked nodes) + “CampusLink” wordmark (Space Grotesk SemiBold, outlined paths)
- Primary brand green: `#075B45`; sage accents `#8EC79A` / `#B7DFC2`
